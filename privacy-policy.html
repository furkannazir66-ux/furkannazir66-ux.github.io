DailyMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Welcome to DailyMate. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how DailyMate handles information when you use our application.

1. Information Collection

DailyMate does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its basic tools.

2. Local Storage

Your notes, tasks and reminders are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not intentionally transmitted to a DailyMate server.

3. Third-Party Services

DailyMate is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information according to its own privacy policy.

4. Data Security

Your locally stored information may be lost if you clear browser data, uninstall your browser or change devices.

5. Children's Privacy

DailyMate does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the application or its features change.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the DailyMate developer through the Pi app.
